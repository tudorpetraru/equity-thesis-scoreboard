Frozen methodology

Rules set before
results arrive.

Version 1 results are never recomputed under future rule changes. Backfilled and voided calls are excluded from headline aggregates.

01

Adjusted daily data

Daily dividend- and split-adjusted closes from Yahoo Finance chart data, with Stooq daily closes as an automatic fallback. Fallback records are flagged as not dividend-adjusted. SPY is the benchmark.

02

Deterministic entry

Entry is the adjusted close of the first trading session whose close occurs at or after generation. SPY uses that same session. There is no intraday selection.

03

Fixed horizons

Six months means 182 calendar days; twelve months means 365. Each horizon uses the last available adjusted close on or before its date.

04

Benchmark verdict

Buy calls are correct when excess return is positive; sell calls when it is negative. Hold is correct inside an inclusive ±5 percentage-point band. Source “Avoid” calls are normalized to strong sell. Conviction is displayed as issued and does not affect scoring.

05

Target and stop

Daily closes are walked forward from entry. The first target crossing or stop breach is shown separately and never changes the correct-or-incorrect verdict.

06

Edge cases

Unmatured horizons remain pending. A delisting or acquisition uses the last available close and is flagged. Missing data is visible and does not break the record.

EXCESS RETURN(Pₕ / P₀ − 1) − (Bₕ / B₀ − 1)

What each term means

P₀
The security’s adjusted closing price on the entry session: the first trading session whose 4:00 p.m. New York close is at or after the thesis generation time.
Pₕ
The security’s adjusted close on the last available trading session at or before the selected horizon date.
B₀
SPY’s adjusted closing price on the same entry session used for P₀.
Bₕ
SPY’s adjusted close on the last available trading session at or before the same horizon date.
h
The horizon being evaluated: 182 calendar days after entry for 6 months, or 365 calendar days after entry for 12 months.

How it is computed. Pₕ / P₀ − 1 is the security’s return. Bₕ / B₀ − 1 is SPY’s return over the corresponding period. Subtracting the SPY return from the security return produces excess return: positive means the security outperformed SPY; negative means it underperformed. The result is calculated separately at 6 and 12 months.

Independent verification

Trust the proof,
not the publisher.

A call is committed publicly while its contents remain hidden. After two days, the record and a random salt are revealed. Recomputing the hash proves the revealed contents match the earlier commitment.

  1. 1

    Find the seal

    Open the history for data/calls.json and locate the commit where the call identifier and commitment first appeared.

  2. 2

    Run the verifier

    python3 scripts/verify.py
python3 scripts/verify.py --call-id <id>
  3. 3

    Confirm the match

    The script canonicalizes the revealed record, adds its salt, and reports whether the SHA-256 commitment matches.
